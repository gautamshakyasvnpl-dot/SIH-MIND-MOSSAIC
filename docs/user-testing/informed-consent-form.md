# Informed Consent Form — SahAIk User Testing (Plain Language)

**Study:** Trying out SahAIk, a learning-support web app built by students for Smart India Hackathon 2026 at USAR, GGSIPU.

## What is this about?
We are students building an app that helps neurodivergent learners with study material. We would like you to try it and tell us what you think.

## What will happen?
1. You will create a test account on our website.
2. You will try 4 short tasks: upload a file, get simplified text, play audio, change settings. About 30–45 minutes total.
3. We may ask you to think aloud while you work.
4. Afterwards you fill a short 10-question survey.

## What data do we collect?
- Your actions in the app (which buttons you pressed, how long tasks took)
- Your survey answers
- Notes we write during the session

We do **not** collect your camera or microphone. We do **not** ask for any diagnosis or medical information.

## How is your data protected?
- Your test account uses a fake email you choose (e.g., tester01@example.com).
- Session notes are stored without your real name — only a participant code.
- All data will be deleted within 30 days after the hackathon report is submitted.

## Do you have to take part?
No. Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any moment, for any reason, without explaining. You can skip any question.

## Will taking part help anyone?
Your feedback directly changes how the app is designed. It helps make the tool better for neurodivergent students like you.
